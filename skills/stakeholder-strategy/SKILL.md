# Stakeholder Strategy

Build an ethical, evidence-based alignment plan.

Map each relevant stakeholder's decision authority, influence, interest, current stance, incentives, concerns, and required commitment. Mark unknowns instead of guessing motivations.

Recommend the decision owner and process, engagement sequence, tailored message and concrete ask for each stakeholder, required evidence, likely objections, dependencies, fallback paths, and how commitments will be recorded.

Prefer direct alignment, transparent tradeoffs, and reversible pilots. Avoid manipulation, covert bypassing, fabricated consensus, or presenting speculation about a person as fact.
